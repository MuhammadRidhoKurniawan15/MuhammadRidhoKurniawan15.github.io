Automatic Spice Drying Machine with ANN Temperature ControlSTM32F446 · ANN · SHT31-F · PZEM-004T · Nextion HMI · TRIAC/TCA785
Final project · PENS · 2025–2026
Closed-loop thermal control system

Developed an STM32F446-based automatic drying system integrating sensing, HMI, power monitoring and ANN-based temperature control.

STM32F446Embedded CANNMATLABSHT31-FPZEM-004TNextionTCA785TRIAC
  • ANN trained with 516 samples using Levenberg–Marquardt.
  • Three ANN inputs: Setpoint, Error and Humidity; 10-neuron hidden layer.
  • Embedded implementation connects ANN output to DAC-based phase-angle control.
  • HMI displays temperature, setpoint, humidity, current, power and frequency in real time.
  • Closed-loop validation performed at 60°C, 80°C and 90°C under disturbance.
