🔬 Portable High-Voltage DC Generator (Final Project)

Undergraduate Final Project · June 2026

This project focuses on the development of a portable high-voltage DC generator for insulation resistance testing of three-phase induction motors. The system employs a Flyback Converter to generate selectable test voltages of 250 VDC, 500 VDC, and 1000 VDC, while an Artificial Neural Network (ANN) running on an STM32F407 microcontroller regulates the PWM duty cycle to maintain stable output voltage under varying input voltage conditions.

Technologies: Flyback Converter · ANN · STM32F407 · Embedded Systems
